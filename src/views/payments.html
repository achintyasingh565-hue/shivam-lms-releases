Record a Payment

Enter a cash, cheque or online payment against a borrower — it updates that loan’s outstanding balance automatically. Pending cheques are saved but don’t reduce the balance until marked cleared.

Charges & Late Fees

One place for every charge on a borrower — late fees, cheque-bounce or other. Charges are added to the outstanding balance and appear in the EMI schedule, reminders and reports. Pick the borrower once, then either apply overdue charges in one click or add a single charge by hand.

1 Apply overdue charges automatically

Adds a late fee for every overdue month and this month’s interest for any fully-missed month, in one click. Repeatable — it never double-charges. Leave Overdue interest blank to work it out from the loan automatically, or type an amount to charge exactly that for every unpaid month.

2 Add a single charge manually

Use this to charge a late fee on a specific date and amount (including months after the tenure), a cheque-bounce fee, or any other charge.
